## About Company
New Al Khan Engineering Works is one of Pakistan's trusted manufacturers of cosmetic and pharmaceutical machinery. With over 25 years of industry experience, we specialize in designing and manufacturing high-quality, reliable, and efficient industrial machines. Our commitment to quality, innovation, and customer satisfaction has enabled us to build long-term relationships with clients across Pakistan. We have proudly worked with many of Pakistan's leading beauty and cosmetics companies, delivering customized machinery and dependable after-sales support.

## Company History
Established over 25 years ago, New Al Khan Engineering Works has become a well-known name in Pakistan's cosmetic and pharmaceutical machinery industry. Throughout our journey, we have successfully manufactured and supplied machines to customers all over Pakistan. Our experience, engineering expertise, and dedication to excellence have earned us the trust of numerous businesses, including several of Pakistan's leading beauty and cosmetics companies. We continue to innovate and provide modern manufacturing solutions that help our clients grow their businesses efficiently.
